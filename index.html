Put this guide on your own site — two lines of HTML
1. Put a container where you want the guide to appear:
<div id="vegan-restaurant-guide">Loading…</div>
2. Load the script once, anywhere on the page:
<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/jzesbaugh/svg-public@main/guide.js"></script>
That's it — no build step, no dependencies, and all styles stay scoped to the container. Keep the placeholder text inside the div; some CMS editors delete empty <div> elements on save.
